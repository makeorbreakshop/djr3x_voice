## What the R-3X Animation build is (from the PNGs, the hardware list and the geometry)

Eight hobby servos on a Pololu Maestro 18, 12 V into buck converters. Parts list
(`07`), resolved from the Amazon links on 2026-09-30:

| qty | item (listing) | used for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wishiot **DS5160SSG 60 kg, 270 deg** | head lift |
| 4 | ZOSKAY **35 kg coreless, 270 deg** (blue) | neck/pan, head tilt, visor, (and a ring) |
| 1 | **20 kg, 180 deg**, dual ball bearing, 25T horn ("double shaft" in the list) | hero arm (shoulder disc) |
| 1 | INJORA **INJS2065 7 kg HV micro** | hero wrist |
| 1 | Pololu Maestro 18-ch | controller (we replace it with our own, sim/docs/motion-control.md 8) |
| 1 / 1 | 6 in lazy susan / 10 in lazy susan | upper neck guide / base pan turntable |
| 2 | MGN12 200 mm rails (cut to 150 and 140 in the CAD) + carriages | lift guide (base) and upper neck guide |
| 1 | F6001ZZ flanged bearing 12x28x8 | visor axle |
| 1 | 8 x 6 x 300 mm aluminium tube | arms |
| 4 | 6 mm rod ends | arm linkages |
| - | M3/M4/M5 button-head assortments, heat-set inserts, 2020 extrusion | fasteners |

The list has 7 servos for 8 channels: 4 x 35 kg "for body rotation, head tilt and visor" +
60 + 20 + 7. The CAD puts a standard-size servo on five channels (neck/base rotation, head
tilt, visor, lower ring, top ring), so the build needs a **fifth 35 kg servo** (or the ring
servos came from elsewhere) - a question for Brandon.

How each joint is driven:

| ch | script name | our joint | drive (geometry) | servo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `neck` | head_pan | the whole neck column turns: a turntable on a 10 in lazy susan in the base; 15T pinion on the turntable walks round a fixed 60T-pitch internal sector (4.00 : 1) | 35 kg 270 |
| 1 | `headlift` | head_lift | 19T pinion on a rack (9.0 mm pitch) on the MGN12 carriage that clamps the neck tube (0.475 mm per servo deg) | 60 kg 270 |
| 2 | `headtilt` | head_tilt | servo on the head plate, 19T pinion rolls on a fixed centre gear on the yoke (1.034 : 1); axis parallel to the visor axle (pitch) | 35 kg 270 |
| 3 | `visor` | visor | horn 25 mm, push rod 58 mm, lever 18 mm on a 12 mm axle in an F6001ZZ; the axle drives the visor arms at the ear cups (~1.39 : 1) | 35 kg 270 |
| 4 | `elbow` | hero_shoulder | dual-shaft servo inside the elbow disc, direct | 20 kg 180 |
| 5 | `hand` | hero_wrist | micro servo in the new wrist, direct roll | 7 kg micro |
| 6 | `lowarm` | torso_lower | 25T pinion on the pedestal top into a 95T-pitch internal sector on the lower ring (3.80 : 1) | 35 kg 270 |
| 7 | `heroarm` | torso_top | 20T pinion into an 85T-pitch internal sector (4.25 : 1) | 35 kg 270 |

The neck tube is guided twice: a 150 mm MGN12 on 2020 in the base (lift) and a 140 mm MGN12
on the 6 in lazy susan at the top ring (keeps the tube upright while it turns). **The head is
carried by the neck column from the base, not by the rings** - turning a ring does not turn
the head (the sim/profile tree says otherwise; see COMPARISON.md).

## Kit parts the motorised build needs modified

From the interference checks (`assembly.py --checks`), the guide transcription and the
builders' notes:

- **Lazy susans** (all three 10 in TamBee): drilled and countersunk to the ring hole patterns
  (guide p20, p33, p52) - already part of the kit build. The R-3X pan stage adds a fourth
  10 in susan and the upper neck guide a 6 in one.
- **LS_IC_1** (static core inside the lower ring): the lower-ring pinion and servo mount sit in
  its wall band (r 98.5-110) - notch or omit it at the servo.
- **Pedestal top (P_M_3 or Henley B_M_3 mod)**: the lower-ring servo hangs through its inner
  lip.
- **Head floor (H_Main_1)**: clamped between the visor frame and the neck plate; its centre
  opening must pass the yoke and clear it over the tilt range.
- **Head shell at the ear line**: the visor axle + adapters stop at x +-93 mm; the kit visor
  arm roots are at +-110..127 and the shell's inner radius is ~97 mm - pierce the shell and join
  the arms inside the ear cups (the build's magenta "visor-demo-arm" is a stand-in).
- **Hero arm**: the build's forearm (mainarm) replaces HA_SB_1 / HA_SP_1 / HA_SP_2, the new
  wrist replaces HA_W_1 / HA_W_2; the kit elbow block (HA_EB_*) and discs (HA_LE_1/RE_1) touch
  the forearm at rest - the elbow block has to be reworked round the servo mount.
- **TR_RR_Full** (pre-2024 download): drill out 4 spots (guide p52). Morton ships a
  `TR-MR_SC_Full_Fixed_More_Clearance` for the top/middle ring seal.
- **Base floor**: the kit base has none; the pan/lift stage needs a plate (Morton: aluminium
  plate + 2020 posts + Lower Frame).

## Bambu project

`bambu/neck animations.3mf` (Brandon's print prep, 2025-06-02): Bambu Lab **H2D**, 0.4 mm
nozzles, 0.16 mm layers, 4 walls, 15 % infill, filaments PETG-CF / ABS / PETG / PLA; plates
hold the head-tilt parts (neck-baseplate, centre gear, neck-main, servo gear) and the visor
parts (base, centre rod, flipper adapter, mount main/cap, push rod, rod tab, servo horn).

## Per-file tables

`role`: *mech* = printed mechanism part; *DNP* = "do not print" (purchased part or reference
stand-in); *shell* = cosmetic kit part. Every R-3X STL has a STEP twin in
`r3x-internal-step/` with the same geometry and frame (bboxes agree to 0.1 mm); the STEPs give the
exact bore axes used as evidence (`out/step_features.json`).
